About

From datasheets to source code

I'm Prabal, an Electrical Engineer who spends as much time thinking about voltage dividers as I do about variables. My training is in circuits and systems, but somewhere along the way I got curious about what happens on the software side of the board — and I haven't stopped since.

These days I split my attention between hardware fundamentals and teaching myself to code, picking up programming and software development one small project at a time. This site is where I'll be documenting that process — equal parts engineering notebook and workbench.

U1
SNN-Controlled SAPF
Final-year project modeling an isolated micro-hydro + solar microgrid in MATLAB/Simulink, then replacing the classical harmonic-filtering math with a Spiking Neural Network built from scratch in Python. The SNN controller matched the classical baseline, cutting grid harmonic distortion from 12.86% down to 4.84% — under the IEEE 519 limit.
